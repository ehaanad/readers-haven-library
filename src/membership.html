QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and changes take effect from your next billing date.

What do I need to register?

A valid photo ID and proof of address. Children under 16 can join with a parent or guardian.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly plans can be cancelled whenever you like, with no cancellation fee.

Can I give membership as a gift?

Absolutely. Gift cards for any paid plan are available at the front desk.
